# DEX Pricing

**DEX is free for everyone.** Every app, every feature, no plans, no tiers, no
trial clock, no credit card, no account required. There is nothing to buy.

## What "free for all" includes

- **The whole suite** — UKI (the assistant), Nexus, Prism, Vinx, Imosion, Flux,
  Axl, Forge, Notes, Tasks, Files, Rewind, the whiteboard, and everything else —
  fully unlocked for every user.
- **All updates**, delivered automatically from the official release feed.
- **Local-first by default.** Your work lives on your device and all apps work
  fully offline.
- **Optional cloud sync**, on your terms — you choose what syncs, if anything.

## How a free product stays funded and keeps improving

There are no ads and no paywalls. The trade for "free for all" is simple and
stated plainly: **DEX uses limited, privacy-protective usage data to improve the
system** — crash reports, performance traces, and which features get used.

- Your **private content is never collected** — passwords, vault items, file
  contents, the clipboard, and full screenshots are off-limits by default, and
  DEX warns you before anything sensitive could be captured.
- **You stay in control.** Turn product analytics on or off, disable cloud sync,
  and export or delete your local data any time.
- DEX does **not** sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Full details: [Privacy Policy](../PRIVACY.md) and [Terms of Use](../TERMS.md), or
in-app under **Settings -> Privacy & Security**.
