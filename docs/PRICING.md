# DEX Pricing

**DEX is paid software.** It is licensed, not sold, under the
[DEX Software License Agreement](../LICENSE). It is not free and not open source.

## Trial

Every new installation gets a **30-day full-feature trial** at no charge: every
app and every feature, no credit card needed. After the trial, continued use
requires a paid licence.

## Licence tiers

| Tier | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal** (subscription) | One person, their own devices | All apps and features, all updates while the subscription is active |
| **Lifetime** (perpetual) | One person | All apps and features for the major version purchased (for example 7.x); updates within that major version |
| **Business** | Teams and organisations | Per-seat licences, the same features, licence terms for commercial use |

Prices are set by the creator and shown at the point of purchase. Licences are
sold by Onazi Treasure OJ or an authorised reseller only.

## What every licence includes

- **The whole suite**: UKI (the assistant), Notes, Tasks, Files, Browser, Maps,
  Terminal, DEX Code, Nexus, the creative apps and everything else.
- **Local first.** Your work lives on your device and the apps work offline.
- **No advertising**, and DEX does not sell your data.

## Privacy

DEX keeps diagnostics on your device. Optional features that go online are
listed in the [Privacy Policy](../PRIVACY.md). Your private content (passwords,
vault items, file contents, the clipboard, screenshots) is never collected.
