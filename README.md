# DEX — your calm, all-in-one desktop

> Created by Onazi Treasure OJ &lt;onazi75@gmail.com&gt; · Version 6.0.0 · Copyright (c) 2026 · DEX Software License Agreement v1.0

**DEX is one app that replaces a dozen — and it's 100% free.** Notes, tasks,
files, a browser, a calendar, drawing, photo and video editing, music, a
knowledge graph, and a built-in AI assistant — all in a single fast, private
desktop workspace that runs entirely on your machine.

One launcher. One assistant. One home for everything you do every day — every
feature unlocked for everyone, no account, no subscription, nothing locked away.

---

## What is DEX, really?

Today your work is scattered: a notes app here, a to-do app there, a photo
editor you barely use, a few cloud accounts, and an AI chatbot in its own place.
DEX folds all of that into **one calm window** — fast, native, and quiet, not a
pile of heavy tabs.

- Press one key, the **assistant (UKI)** opens. Tell it what you want in plain
  words — "summarize this", "open my budget", "make a 3-step reminder" — and it
  does it, showing you a plan first for anything risky.
- Every tool — notes, files, drawing, video, music — lives **inside DEX** and
  shares context, so your work follows you instead of getting stranded in
  separate apps.
- **Nothing leaves your machine** unless you say so. No required account.
  Your data is yours.

If you've ever wished your computer just felt like *one calm place to get things
done*, that's DEX.

## Why people use DEX

- **One assistant for everything.** Open UKI, type what you want, get it done —
  no menu hunting across a dozen apps.
- **Plans you can preview.** Anything that changes your files or system is shown
  to you first as a clear list of steps. You hit **Run** or **Cancel**.
- **Apps that work together.** Notes, Tasks, Files, the creative suite, Rewind,
  and more share context, so a file you open in one shows up where you need it.
- **Yours, on your machine.** History, patterns, and shortcuts stay on this
  device. Cloud sync is optional. Analytics is off by default.
- **Built for keyboards.** Every action has a shortcut. `Ctrl+K` launcher,
  `/` command menu, `Ctrl+Space` for the assistant, `Ctrl+Shift+M` for compact
  mode.
- **Free, and light.** Every feature is unlocked for everyone, and DEX is built
  to stay quiet on your machine — one calm tool, not tab sprawl.

## What's new in 6.0

6.0 turns DEX into a real desktop, not just an app — a calm, OS-grade shell with
proper windows, a faster core, and quiet polish end to end.

- **Real window management.** Floating windows you can drag, snap, and tile —
  side-by-side halves, quarters, columns/rows, and Master/Dwindle/Monocle layouts,
  all from the keyboard or the command palette.
- **Custom snap zones.** A FancyZones-style editor (`Ctrl+Alt+Z`): draw your own
  zones, save layouts, drag a window to snap it, and grow it across zones with
  `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Arrow`.
- **Calmer, more accessible.** Bigger, easier hit targets, motion that scales with
  the element and respects your "reduce motion" setting, and notifications that can
  stay put when they matter (alarms, calls) instead of vanishing.
- **A smarter assistant.** UKI now prefers on-device models for quick tasks and
  falls back to the cloud only when needed — faster, more private replies.
- **Safer add-ons.** Store apps run capability-scoped and sandboxed, so an add-on
  only gets the access it declares — nothing more.
- **Faster, more reliable.** Heavy apps load only when opened, the packaged app
  start-up was cut down, and a batch of real crash/visual fixes landed.

## Your data, in plain words

DEX is free for everyone. To keep it improving, DEX uses **limited, privacy-
protective usage data** — and never your private files or secrets. No ads, no
selling your data, no paywalled features.

- **Your work stays on your computer.** Notes, files, and your screen live on
  your device; they are not uploaded by default.
- **DEX collects limited diagnostics and usage signals** — crashes, performance,
  and which features get used — to fix problems and make the system better. This
  is how a free product keeps getting better, and it's the trade for "free for
  all."
- **Your private content is never collected.** Passwords, vault items, file
  contents, the clipboard, and full screenshots are off-limits by default, and
  DEX warns you before anything sensitive could be captured.
- **The assistant runs on your machine.** No required cloud account. Cloud sync is
  optional — you choose what syncs, if anything.
- **You stay in control.** Turn product analytics on or off, disable cloud sync,
  and export or delete your local data any time. Permissions (microphone, camera,
  screen, files, clipboard) ask first, then remember your answer.
