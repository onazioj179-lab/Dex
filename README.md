# DEX — your calm, all-in-one desktop

> Created by Onazi Treasure OJ &lt;onazi75@gmail.com&gt; · Version 5.0.1 · Copyright (c) 2026 · DEX Software License Agreement v1.0

**DEX is one app that replaces a dozen.** Notes, tasks, files, a browser, a
calendar, drawing, photo and video editing, music, a knowledge graph, and a
built-in AI assistant — all in a single fast, private desktop workspace that
runs entirely on your machine.

One launcher. One assistant. One home for everything you do every day — without
juggling tabs, accounts, or subscriptions scattered across the web.

---

## What is DEX, really?

Think of your computer today: a browser with 40 tabs, a notes app, a to-do app,
a photo editor you barely use, three cloud accounts, and an AI chatbot in yet
another tab. DEX folds all of that into **one window**.

- Press one key, the **assistant (UKI)** opens. Tell it what you want in plain
  words — "summarize this", "open my budget", "make a 3-step reminder" — and it
  does it, showing you a plan first for anything risky.
- Every tool — notes, files, drawing, video, music — lives **inside DEX** and
  shares context, so your work follows you instead of getting stranded in
  separate apps.
- **Nothing leaves your machine** unless you say so. No required account.
  Your data is yours.

If you've ever wished your computer just felt like *one calm place to get things
done*, that's DEX.

---

## Why people use DEX

- **One assistant for everything.** Open UKI, type what you want, get it done —
  no menu hunting across a dozen apps.
- **Plans you can preview.** Anything that changes your files or system is shown
  to you first as a clear list of steps. You hit **Run** or **Cancel**.
- **Apps that work together.** Notes, Tasks, Files, the creative suite, Rewind,
  and more share context, so a file you open in one shows up where you need it.
- **Yours, on your machine.** History, patterns, and shortcuts stay on this
  device. Cloud sync is optional. Analytics is off by default.
- **Built for keyboards.** Every action has a shortcut. `Ctrl+K` launcher,
  `/` command menu, `Ctrl+Space` for the assistant.
- **One price, no tab sprawl.** Replace several paid subscriptions with one tool
  that works offline.

## What's inside

DEX is one home for many small, focused apps. Open them from the launchpad,
search with `Ctrl+K`, or pin them to the Stage strip on the side.

**Daily** — Home · Notes · Tasks · Files · Browser · Calendar · Workflows

**Knowledge & focus** — Nexus (knowledge graph) · Learn (flashcards & spaced
review) · Rewind (timeline of your day) · Reader (distraction-free pages)

**Creative** — Vinx (drawing) · Imosion (music studio) · Flux (photo editing) ·
Prism (live visuals) · Forge (on-device image generation) · AXL (2D/3D drafting)
· Canvas (whiteboard) · PresentationBuilder · PixelArt · PDFViewer · Kanban ·
DexCode (code scratchpad)

**Play & store** — Games · Store (safe, reviewed add-ons you install in one click)

**The assistant** — **UKI**: press `Ctrl+Space`, say what you want. It drafts,
summarizes, opens things, and chains short workflows — always showing a plan
first for anything that changes your data.

## Your privacy, in plain words

- Your work lives **on your computer**. Notes, files, and your screen are not
  uploaded anywhere by default.
- The assistant runs **on your machine**. No required cloud account.
- **Cloud sync is optional** — you choose what syncs, if anything.
- **Analytics is off by default.** Turn it on and it's anonymous; turn it off any
  time.
- **Permissions ask first.** Microphone, camera, screen capture, files, and the
  clipboard each prompt the first time, then remember your answer.
- **You control your data with clear, opt-in settings. Paid plans are ad-free.**
